# Airport turn SLAs

The target is the maximum total turn time for the airport. Total turn time is measured from the completed `aircraftArrival` event to the completed `aircraftDeparture` event.

| Airport | Airport code | Target turn time |
|---|---|---:|
| London Gatwick | `LGW` | 40 minutes |
| London Luton | `LTN` | 35 minutes |
| Manchester | `MAN` | 42 minutes |
| Bristol | `BRS` | 38 minutes |
| Amsterdam Schiphol | `AMS` | 45 minutes |
